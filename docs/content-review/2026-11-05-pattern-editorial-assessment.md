All entries were read for unsupported factual/predictive claims, reader agency, intrusive assumptions, chapter relevance, distinctness and Reflection's quiet editorial tone. Prompts ask readers to notice, consider or choose; they do not predict events, diagnose a person, mandate a relationship decision or promise an outcome. Connection includes non-romantic relationships and reciprocal boundaries. Rest avoids treating productivity as a prerequisite for rest. Growth offers small experiments and leaves room to stop or revise.

The shared opening construction makes the collection consistent but somewhat formulaic. This is a non-blocking editorial tradeoff: each entry still has a distinct focus and prompt. The short entries should be described as reflective chapter starting points, not comprehensive natal interpretations. The computed sign label and the symbolic prose remain separate.
